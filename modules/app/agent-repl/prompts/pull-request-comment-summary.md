Now write the shortened comment.

Your whole reply is ONE SHORT SENTENCE of plain text, at most twelve words,
and nothing else. No preamble, no quotes, no backticks, no markdown, no label,
and never the commenter's name, which the notification already shows.

Say what the commenter asks for, objects to, or reports, naming its concrete
subject, so the reader knows at a glance whether to look now. Leave out
greetings, pleasantries, code, links, and anything the reader would have to
open the pull request to understand.

Keep the sentence short by choosing what matters, never by packing clauses
together. Use no em dashes, en dashes, semicolons, or parentheses.

Good shortenings look like these:
Asks to rename the flag before merging.
Reports the migration fails on an empty table.
Approves once the flaky test is fixed.
Questions why the cache is cleared on every read.
